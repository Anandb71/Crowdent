# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Focusing on what is best for the research community
- Showing empathy and respect
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Taking safety claims seriously and refusing to overstate readiness

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Asking for help to bypass safety gates, actuate hardware, or collect
  device identifiers

## Enforcement

Report issues to the repository maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers may
warn, limit, or ban contributors whose behaviour they judge harmful.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
